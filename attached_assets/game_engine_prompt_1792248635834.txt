#### 📐 Core Game Loop & Mathematical Matrix
While you have complete freedom over the visuals, the core underlying logical architecture must strictly adhere to these mechanics:

*   **The Deck:** Exactly 15 total cards. 5 unique suits: Spades (♠), Hearts (♥), Diamonds (♦), Clubs (♣), and Demon (👿). There are exactly 3 identical cards per suit. Cards have no numerical values; they are identified purely by their suit symbol/vector.
*   **The Target:** At the start of each round, 1 card is pulled from the deck array and stored as the hidden `targetCard`. The sole objective of the match is for a team to deduce this specific suit vector.
*   **Shared Hands:** 3 cards are dealt to Team A, and 3 cards are dealt to Team B. These cards belong to the team universally as a shared container array, not to individual player slots.
*   **Turn Rotation:** A strict 4-player rotation loop: Team A Player 1 → Team B Player 1 → Team A Player 2 → Team B Player 2.
*   **Action Flow:** On their turn, an active player can either:
    1.  **Reveal & Draw:** Move 1 to 3 selected cards from their team hand into a public `cemetery` log, automatically drawing replacements from the deck.
    2.  **Submit Guess:** Formally guess the hidden target suit. A correct guess wins the round instantly. An incorrect guess flashes an error state, deactivates that suit option globally for future guesses, and passes the turn.

#### 🥷 The Hidden Rating & Equalization Engine
*   **Invisible Points System:** Players must never see their current numerical scores or points anywhere on the user interface. Keep this data entirely hidden in the backend state.
    *   *Reveal Action:* +10 points to the active player's round score.
    *   *Correct Guess:* +50 points to both teammates on the winning team.
    *   *Incorrect Guess:* -30 points strictly from the active player who guessed incorrectly.
    *   *Round End Formula:* Calculate a recency-weighted rating: `newRating = (pointsThisRound * 0.7) + (previousRating * 0.3)`.
*   **Host Rebalance Matrix:** The host interface must feature a specific "Equalize & Rebalance Matrix" button. When triggered at the end of a round, the engine must silently execute a mandatory structural rebuild within the current room session:
    1.  Sort all 4 connected players by their computed performance `rating` from highest to lowest.
    2.  Automatically assign Rank 1 (highest) and Rank 4 (lowest) to form the new Team A.
    3.  Automatically assign Rank 2 and Rank 3 to form the new Team B.
    4.  Flush all hands, reset the deck array pointer, and instantly transition to kick off the next round seamlessly within the exact same session container.
