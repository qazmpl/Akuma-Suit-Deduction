# Akuma Deduction — animated film script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Four players can turn a shared hand into a race to name one hidden suit.
- **Identity:** A noir tabletop film: ink-black felt, bone paper, restrained brass, and one slash of vermilion. Display face: Cormorant Garamond; supporting face: DM Sans; annotations: IBM Plex Mono.
- **Hero:** The game's physical language—five suit marks, a sealed target card, and two shared three-card hands—treated like evidence on a midnight table.
- **Motion:** Card-scale match cuts, split-table camera reframes, and evidence-line tracing.
- **Arc:** Suspicion snaps into focus; the rules accelerate through a measured turn cycle; the ending resolves into a calm title lockup.
- **Payoff:** The four player positions collapse into a single target card, then the hidden mark appears as a match cut back to the opening seal.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 24 seconds.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.15s / 4.15s — “FIVE SUITS. ONE SECRET.”

**Purpose:** Make the hidden target and five possible suits legible immediately; establish tactile tension.

**Exact content:** At 0.00, a small mono eyebrow reads “A 2v2 SUIT-DEDUCTION GAME” at upper-left and holds through 3.70. At 0.35, the phrase “FIVE SUITS.” appears in oversized cream Cormorant at center-left; at 1.25 it is replaced by “ONE SECRET.” in the same fixed baseline. Five suit marks appear one at a time beneath the headline: “♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ ✦”, 1.70–2.20. At 2.45, “The target is hidden.” appears lower-left and holds until 3.70.

**Composition:** Open on an almost full-frame ivory card back, rotated -8 degrees and cropped on the right. A black circular seal sits just off-centre; black felt and a thin brass frame sit behind it. Text owns left 46%; card owns right 54%. Flecks of red paper fiber remain sparse and still.

**Artistic idea:** The target seal is a physical lens: five suit marks orbit its edge, but the center stays blank. “ONE SECRET” appears as if stamped through the seal, not as a floating title.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 card back is already in frame, oversized and slightly blurred; a second on-screen element—the thin gold outline word “AKUMA”—is visible behind it. 0.12–0.38 card snaps into sharp focus with stiff spring and settles. 0.48–0.95 five suit glyphs fire in separated 100ms beats along a shallow arc. 1.05–1.35 card yaws 4 degrees; headline replaces without layout movement. 1.50–2.15 red ring traces around the blank target seal; the five glyphs stop. 2.35–3.15 camera punches toward the seal while the subtitle resolves. 3.20–4.00 the ring expands and crosses frame; it is moving into the next shot's tabletop split, not holding.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing red ring is the incoming scene's left-team boundary. Card stays physically continuous through the cut; shot 02 starts with the same seal at the same scale, then the camera pulls back.

**Assets/technique:** CSS/SVG card, suit glyphs and evidence ring; layered felt texture made from restrained CSS; no external artwork required.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental starts with a low pulse. Card snap on first accent, then five subtle rhythmic taps; planned accents are unverified until track analysis.

### Shot 02 / 4.15–9.15s / 5.00s — “TWO TEAMS. ONE SHARED HAND.”

**Purpose:** Explain the four-player, 2v2 structure and shared team hand without presenting a static rule slide.

**Exact content:** “2 TEAMS” appears upper-left at 4.35; “2 PLAYERS EACH” replaces it at 5.25. Team labels “A” and “B” remain on left/right sides. At 6.15, a center caption reads “A shared hand. A shared read.” and holds through 8.55. Player positions are labeled “A1”, “A2”, “B1”, “B2”.

**Composition:** Opens close on the ring/card from shot 01. Camera pulls to an overhead felt table divided into two asymmetrical halves by a fine brass line. Team A occupies left third in warm parchment/gold; Team B occupies right third in cool sage. Two seat markers sit at each outer edge; their shared three-card hand sits closer to center. No UI chrome.

**Artistic idea:** The two shared hands are mirrored constellations connected by a single evidence line; their cards are backs, making the hidden information shared within each team rather than individually held.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45 red ring becomes a dividing line while the camera pulls back. 0.35–0.80 A1 and B1 card-seat markers click into opposing corners; A2/B2 answer at 0.85–1.25. 1.10–1.70 each team's three card-backs assemble from a split fan, one after another, angled toward the middle. 1.80–2.40 header changes to “2 PLAYERS EACH”; team labels anchor. 2.55–3.40 a fine line draws from each seat to its shared hand, then one line bridges across center and stops short of the sealed target. 3.55–4.20 caption appears while hands tilt inward, as if both teams are studying the same table. 4.25–5.00 three card-backs peel toward camera and fill frame.

**Transition contract:** A card back from each shared hand rotates toward lens; their matching dark backs merge into the first card of the 15-card inventory in shot 03. Use scale-through plus a two-panel seam closing, not a dissolve.

**Assets/technique:** CSS card backs, typography, SVG evidence strokes; use perspective on groups but keep individual cards' rotation restrained.

**Sound intent:** Pulse opens into hand percussion; seat markers and card assembly land on accents, not presumed beats.

### Shot 03 / 9.15–14.00s / 4.85s — “15 CARDS. 5 SUITS.”

**Purpose:** Convey the deck count and setup: one random target, three cards per team, eight remaining.

**Exact content:** At 9.30 “15” appears very large; at 10.05 “CARDS” resolves beside it. Five suit stacks receive labels “3 EACH” at 10.55: spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs, demon. At 11.65 a card is dealt face-down into a brass target frame; label “1 RANDOM TARGET” appears. At 12.65, two groups of three card backs move to the two teams; “3 SHARED EACH” appears. At 13.45, eight card backs remain stacked; “8 IN THE DRAW PILE” appears and holds to 13.85.

**Composition:** Graphic top-down inventory on warm bone paper, unlike the prior dark felt wide. Five narrow suit columns occupy the upper 60%; their stacks are precisely three cards deep and each has a distinct suit color restrained to the suit symbol only. Lower portion is a dark felt trough receiving the target and team hands. The large “15” sits lower-left in black, not as a header wrapper.

**Artistic idea:** The deck is counted in one continuous deal: three cards per suit physically separate, one slips into the target frame, three go to each team, and eight visibly remain. The arithmetic is the animation.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.30 incoming card face fills frame; its back flips into a top-down face. 0.25–0.85 five suit stacks assemble in a 0.12s stagger, three layers each; the giant “15” stamps on the beat. 0.90–1.50 top cards lift to expose each suit mark, then settle. 1.55–2.35 one card travels down the center lane into target frame, flips face-down at the last instant; label locks beside it. 2.45–3.35 six cards split to the two team zones, three to each in mirrored triplets. 3.50–4.20 remaining eight cards sweep into lower-right; count increments in visible steps 1–8. 4.25–5.00 camera dives into the eight-card pile; a card edge becomes a turn-order track.

**Transition contract:** The eight-card pile's long edge extends diagonally into four seat markers in shot 04. A cream paper panel folds away along that same edge as the camera dives; target frame remains as a small persistent red ring.

**Assets/technique:** CSS/SVG card stack geometry and suit typography; no photography. Individual cards are actual graphic cards with border, index and large center glyph.

**Sound intent:** Percussive counting follows the deal; card 8 kick/low accent aligned to transition if track onset permits.

### Shot 04 / 14.00–19.00s / 5.00s — “A1 → B1 → A2 → B2”

**Purpose:** Make the repeating turn order and legal turn actions intuitive.

**Exact content:** Turn sequence “A1 → B1 → A2 → B2” appears across the frame at 14.25. Player names remain seat codes only. At 15.05, one to three cards leave Team A's shared hand; caption “Reveal 1–3. Draw the same number.” appears at 15.45 and holds through 18.60. At 17.20, one incorrect suit mark gets a red strike; “Wrong suits close for everyone.” appears at 17.55.

**Composition:** The camera begins tight on the diagonal eight-card edge and races along it to a four-position horizontal turn track. Mid-shot punches into Team A's shared hand; the rest of the table is darkened but visible. Final reframe shows the five suit marks in a horizontal row at lower center.

**Artistic idea:** Turn order is a moving spotlight: four seat codes pulse around the table, then the lens dives into the active team's hand. A card is revealed outward and replaced from the deck; the empty space tells the rule.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55 card edge elongates to the turn track; camera follows the line. 0.55–1.10 A1 pulses gold, then light travels to B1, A2, B2 in 0.17s taps; sequence loops once at reduced speed. 1.30–1.80 camera punches to Team A's three backs; one, then two, then three cards lift in a quick count preview. 1.90–2.65 one card flips to reveal a suit glyph; matching card draws from pile into the open slot. 2.75–3.35 caption traces in along a line from the hand; the slot closes. 3.45–4.10 a selected wrong suit mark is stamped vermilion and crossed; other three marks dim, not disappear. 4.15–5.00 red strike travels toward the next scene's final-choice row.

**Transition contract:** The moving red strike becomes a slash across the wrong suit in shot 05. Four remaining suit choices match position and size at cut; target card frame stays at center.

**Assets/technique:** SVG turn path and evidence-line draw, CSS perspective cards, focus dimming; clear multi-plane composition.

**Sound intent:** Tight ticks on seat progression, low card flip on reveal, clipped impact on red strike; cut alignment unverified until bed analyzed.

### Shot 05 / 19.00–24.00s / 5.00s — “GUESS RIGHT. WIN TOGETHER.”

**Purpose:** Resolve the rules into the stakes and deliver a distinctive brand ending.

**Exact content:** At 19.30 “GUESS THE SUIT.” appears above five large suit marks. The incorrect suit already marked in shot 04 stays crossed; at 20.15 another wrong mark gets a cross and becomes disabled to everyone. At 21.00 the last eligible mark snaps into the target frame; exact copy “Correct guess. Your team wins.” appears at 21.45. At 22.35 “AKUMA DEDUCTION” appears as the title lockup in Cormorant, with small “A 2v2 HIDDEN-SUIT GAME” beneath. All final copy holds through 23.70. Do not imply special abilities or expanded Akuma mode.

**Composition:** Palette inverts to parchment, with the target card large and centered (short edge ~68% of frame); black suit glyph in the middle. Five suits form a shallow arc behind it. Title occupies bottom quarter, right aligned to the centered card's edge. No CTA.

**Artistic idea:** Wrong guesses do not vanish; they become permanent evidence, crossing themselves out. The winning mark cuts through the evidence row and locks into the target seal. Then the noisy investigation folds into the spare, assured title card.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 red slash continues across the first wrong suit. 0.20–0.75 “GUESS THE SUIT” unmasks through a narrow horizontal aperture. 0.90–1.45 second wrong suit crosses out with a two-part strike; remaining eligible suits hold their color. 1.50–2.20 winning suit scales 0.4→1.12→1.0 and physically enters the target seal; paper fibers ripple outward. 2.25–2.90 caption resolves beneath the seal; all other suits dim to etched grey. 3.00–3.55 suit row folds behind card; the card turns to face front and shrinks slightly as the black title field expands around it. 3.60–4.25 title letters lock together; the small descriptor appears 0.2s after. 4.25–5.00 title stays readable while the gold seal contracts to the opening shot's size and offset, carrying motion directly into the loop.

**Transition contract:** Final gold seal shrinks to an off-centre dark circle; the next loop's opening card back is already visible inside that circle. Opening and ending share exact circle position, scale and brass edge for a seamless restart.

**Assets/technique:** CSS/SVG paper, card, symbol, seal, type and evidence strike; palette inversion by scene field change, not a white flash.

**Sound intent:** Beat-led deductions build to a warm low-frequency resolve at title, with last note sustaining across the loop handoff. No voiceover or SFX requested.
